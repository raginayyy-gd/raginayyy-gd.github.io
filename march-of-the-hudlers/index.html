March of the Hudlers Campaign branding

March of the Hudlers was the campaign for Huddle01's Testnet launch. The team wanted it Agora-themed, in line with the UI of the Testnet. The branding revolved around classical paintings, dramatic compositions and exaggerated type: bold condensed headings set against centuries-old art. Prussian blue paired with beige rounded it out.

During the node sale, purchasers were randomly allocated illustrated cards as NFTs, each with a rarity tier marked by stars. The gamified system turned node purchasing into something closer to opening a loot box, adding anticipation and collectability and urging users to buy more.
