Ethereum Unblocked SF Brand strategy & event identity

Rooted in San Francisco, the identity draws from two sources: the Golden Gate Bridge, reread from an oblique angle to derive the Ethereum diamond, and the optical behaviour of vintage display screens. Those screens render colour through three discrete phosphor dots. That subpixel logic became the brand's visual language.

The bleeding, overlapping RGB channels you see across every asset became the system. Typography, 3D objects, motion and colour, everything runs through the same filter, kept deliberately consistent.
