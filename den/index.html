DEN Brand design

Started by an ex-colleague, I was hired as Creative Director to design the brand identity of DEN, an onchain streaming platform built for degen creators.

The job was to reflect the raw, high-contrast, punk-influenced ethos: black and white with red accents, textures and type that feel pulled from a gig poster. It works because the raw messaging matches the product.
