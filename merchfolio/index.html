Merchfolio Merch for Huddle01

My approach to merch for Huddle01 has always been intuitive and contextual. The designs are based on the event they're made for, the country they'll be worn in, and the community they'll be given to.

I try to keep merch as open-ended as possible relative to the brand. It acts as a gateway: people get intrigued and develop interest in the product from nothing more than a design on the back of a T-shirt.
