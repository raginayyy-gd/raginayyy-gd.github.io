Ethereum Unblocked SF

Event identity for Ethereum Unblocked SF, with Orok for QED and the Ethereum Foundation. Rooted in San Francisco: the Golden Gate Bridge reread from an oblique angle into the Ethereum diamond, and the subpixel RGB logic of vintage display screens applied to every asset.

Build India

Identity for Build India, an in-person hackathon in Bengaluru by Anthropic, Replit and Lightspeed, produced with Devfolio. The logo recomposes Claude's geometric shapes into the letterforms of BUILD INDIA. Black and orange: grounded enough for stage and large format, flexible enough for merch.

Trustless Conference

Brand for Trustless, the Ethereum Foundation conference at Devconnect Buenos Aires 2025. Y2K design marked the internet's first great leap; Trustless carries the same weight for web3. The visual language is pulled from that era of digital optimism and rebuilt through a current lens, across graphics, website, merch and stage.

Blocktrain

India's first hackathon on a moving train. Bengaluru to New Delhi, 36 hours, builders onboard. A train-shaped logotype, Indian Railways stamp typography, vintage ticket textures and hand-drawn illustration, extended into speaker cards styled as tickets, boarding-pass posters, totes and tees.

DEN

Brand identity for DEN, an onchain streaming platform built for degen creators. Raw, high-contrast and punk-influenced: black and white with red accents, textures and type that feel pulled from a gig poster, because the raw messaging matches the product's ethos.

March of the Hudlers

Campaign branding for Huddle01's Testnet launch. Agora-themed to match the Testnet UI: classical paintings, dramatic compositions and bold condensed type set in Prussian blue and beige. During the node sale, purchasers were allocated illustrated cards as NFTs with star-rated rarity tiers.

ETH India 2024

Identity for ETH India 2024 by Devfolio, drawn from ancient Indian board games: snaking paths, geometric patterns and hand-illustrated motifs. A game where everyone starts at the same square. The system expanded into volunteer applications, partner cards, UI elements and physical merch in a warm, folk-art palette.

Merchfolio

Merch for Huddle01 across several events. Each design is contextual: the event it's made for, the country it'll be worn in, the community it's given to. Kept open-ended relative to the brand, so a design on the back of a t-shirt acts as a gateway into the product.

Ragy

Ragy is an independent designer with a focus on brand design, with experience across marketing, branding and merchandise. Her work lives in the space between a brief and its best possible outcome, translating intent into visuals that land exactly as imagined. She moves easily across formats, shaped more by the work than by a single discipline.

Based in India. Available remotely worldwide.
