Trustless Conference Conference branding

Rooted in Buenos Aires during Devconnect 2025, Trustless is an Ethereum Foundation conference built around one idea: an open, censorship-resistant internet that needs no middleman.

Y2K design marked the internet's first great leap. Trustless carries the same weight for web3. That parallel shaped the brand: the visual language is pulled directly from that era of digital optimism, then rebuilt through a current lens to feel inevitable rather than nostalgic. It translated from graphics to website to merch to stage.
