Build India Hackathon branding

Build India was an in-person hackathon in Bengaluru by Anthropic, Replit and Lightspeed, focused on building AI-native products. The logo is built from Claude's geometric shapes, recomposed into the letterforms of BUILD INDIA, so the brief is baked directly into the identity.

Black and orange: grounded enough for stage and large format, flexible enough for merch.
