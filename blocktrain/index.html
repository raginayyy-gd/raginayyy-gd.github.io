Blocktrain A travelling hackathon

Blocktrain was India's first hackathon on a moving train. Bengaluru to New Delhi, 36 hours, builders onboard. The logo takes the shape of a train, with letterforms structured around the shape of an engine.

The visual language draws from Indian Railways: stamp typography, vintage ticket textures, hand-drawn illustration. Familiar, nostalgic and distinctly Indian. The system extended into speaker cards styled as tickets, registration posters as boarding passes, tote bags and T-shirts. Everything designed to feel like it belonged on that train.
