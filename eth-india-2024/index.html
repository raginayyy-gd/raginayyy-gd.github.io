ETH India 2024 Playing the infinite game

The ETH India 2024 identity draws from the visual language of ancient Indian board games. The snaking paths, geometric patterns and hand-illustrated motifs all reference the tactile, communal nature of games like Snakes & Ladders. It felt like the right metaphor for a hackathon: a game where everyone starts at the same square.

From there the system expanded into a full suite of assets: volunteer applications, partner cards, UI elements and physical merch, all holding the same warm, folk-art-inspired palette. The challenge was making something that felt distinctly Indian without leaning on cliché, and letting the illustration style do that heavy lifting across every touchpoint.
